## How to sign

You agree to this Agreement by submitting a contribution together with a sign-off line in your
commit message certifying that you have read and agree to it:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Add it automatically with `git commit -s`. By including this line you confirm that you have read
and agree to this Contributor License Agreement and to the Developer Certificate of Origin below.

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** If you are contributing as part of your employment or
otherwise on behalf of an organization, please have an authorized representative contact
team@scancrypt.org so we can put a Corporate CLA in place.
