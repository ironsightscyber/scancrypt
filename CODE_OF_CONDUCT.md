# Code of Conduct

ScanCrypt is a tool people reach for on one of the worst days of their working
life. The project's spaces should be safe for a stressed IT admin at an
encrypted business, a first-time contributor, and a seasoned responder alike.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, pull
requests, discussions), project email (team@scancrypt.org and its aliases),
and anywhere you are representing the project.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and claims, not people.
- Be patient, especially with users asking for help mid-incident. Questions
  that look basic are often being asked under real pressure.
- Stay honest. The project never overstates what it can recover; hold your
  comments and contributions to the same standard.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Posting victim-identifying material: real company or server names,
  ransom-note contents with contact details, encrypted files, or keys.
  Redact first — the project only ever needs family-level indicators
  (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Spam, deliberate derailing, or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to **team@scancrypt.org**. Reports are read
by the maintainer, handled confidentially, and acknowledged within a few
business days. Depending on severity, responses range from a warning to
removal of comments to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
The maintainer may remove victim-identifying material on sight.
